# ActiveQ Governance v21.0

Active Query (synonymous with and wheresoever abbreviated as "ActiveQ" or "AQ", whether upper cased, 
lower cased or capitalised) and applies to all parts of ActiveQ projects, programmes, respositeries, and spaces. 
In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment ActiveQ Governance Framework has been adopted 
in persuance of envisioning and enforcing policies, licenses, agreements and guidelines;

* [Ethical Source Policy][ESP]

* [Code of Conduct Policy][COCP]

Above policy documents are available through files placed at the root directory of each ActiveQ repository and 
are also available through web access at

[https://activeq.github.io/governance/][WEB] or 

[https://github.io/ActiveQ/Governance/][WEBGIT]


Further details of specific policies and their enforcement may be posted separately.
